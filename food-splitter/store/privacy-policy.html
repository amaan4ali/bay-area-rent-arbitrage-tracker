FoodSplitter Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2, 2026

1. Information We Collect

FoodSplitter collects the following information to provide our bill-splitting service:

  • Account Information: Phone number, display name, and profile photo when you create an account.
  • Payment Information: When you link a payment method, your card or bank details are stored securely by Stripe, our payment processor. We never store your full card number on our servers.
  • Meal Data: Bill items, amounts, and split calculations you create within the app.
  • Receipt Images: Photos of receipts you scan are processed for text extraction and stored temporarily for the duration of the meal split.
  • Contacts: With your permission, we access your contacts to help you find friends on FoodSplitter. Contact data is not stored on our servers.
  • Device Information: Push notification tokens, device type, and OS version for delivering notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate and display bill splits between you and your friends.
  • To process payments between users via Stripe Connect.
  • To send push notifications about payment requests and confirmations.
  • To scan receipts and extract item data using optical character recognition (OCR).
  • To help you find friends who also use FoodSplitter.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, and cloud storage. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Stripe: Payment processing. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Google Cloud Vision: Receipt text extraction. Google Cloud Privacy
  • Expo: Push notifications and app updates. Expo Privacy Policy

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only:

  • With other FoodSplitter users you choose to split bills with (limited to display name and split amounts).
  • With Stripe to process payments.
  • When required by law or to protect our rights.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • Meal and payment records are retained for 2 years for your records and dispute resolution.
  • Receipt images are deleted 30 days after a meal is settled.
  • You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • All data in transit is encrypted via TLS/SSL.
  • Firebase Security Rules restrict data access to authorized users only.
  • Payment data is handled entirely by Stripe (PCI DSS Level 1 compliant).
  • Receipt images are stored in private Firebase Storage buckets.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored in the app.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Opt out of push notifications via device settings.
  • Revoke contacts access via device settings.

8. Children's Privacy

FoodSplitter is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via push notification or in-app notice.
